Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

On-device processing

PDF Unlock processes documents directly on your device. The app does not transmit your PDF files, their passwords, or their contents to Grégoire Thomas or any third-party service.

Data collection

PDF Unlock does not collect, retain, or share personal data, usage data, or diagnostic data.

File access

The app accesses only the files and folders you select. Original files are not modified. Unlocked copies are placed in an unlocked subfolder authorized by the user.

Third-party services

The app does not include analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party SDKs that collect data.

Contact

For questions about this policy or data processing, email grthoma@gmail.com.
